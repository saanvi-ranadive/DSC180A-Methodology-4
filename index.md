**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

I have found the most interesting topic covered so far in my domain to be Fractal Chain-of-Thought, because it shows what a huge impact a more sophisticated prompting technique can have on LLM output. **2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

One potential investigation I would like to pursue would be to develop a veracity engine that evaluates misinformation in the media. We could build on our current work with Fractal Chain-of-Thought (F-CoT) and explore additional prompting techniques to analyze media content through political, sentiment, and economic lenses. We would also continue refining our veracity vector and factuality factor framework as the foundation of the engine, investigating how effectively these factors can be used to identify and evaluate different forms of misinformation.  

**3. What is a potential change you’d make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

Throughout Quarter 1, we have spent a lot of time exploring and experimenting with different prompting techniques and approaches to evaluating misinformation. For Quarter 2, I think our main focus should be to establish a more specific research objective and build on our findings from Quarter 1 to work toward that goal. Rather than continuing with broad exploration, we could take a more structured approach by focusing our experiments on addressing a particular research question and evaluating how effectively our methods achieve that objective.  

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be very interested in exploring other prompting techniques such as Tree of Thought (ToT) and self-consistency, to see how they compare to FCoT. Specifically, I would like to investigate whether these techniques lead to more reliable or consistent outputs than FCoT, or whether combining them with FCoT could lead to improved results.
